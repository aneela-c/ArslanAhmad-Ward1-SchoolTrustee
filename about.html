MEET ARSLAN

YOUR VOICE MATTERS. LET'S BUILD STRONGER SCHOOLS TOGETHER.

Arslan Ahmad is a proud resident of the King/Ward 1 community. Having called Kleinburg home and now residing in King, with family throughout Ward 1 and King City, his commitment to this community runs deep.

As an educated business professional and part of a successful family-run business, Arslan understands hard work, responsibility, accountability, and investing in the future of our kids.

He is running for School Trustee to ask the difficult questions, examine the evidence, and work toward better outcomes—so every student can reach their full potential.

WHO I AM AS A CANDIDATE

A NEIGHBOUR WHO SHOWS UP.

01

Your local neighbour

I don’t just represent this community—I live here. I care deeply about the people and families who call it home. The challenges our community faces matter to me because they affect my neighbours, my family, and the future of our children. That is why I am committed to making our schools stronger for every student.

02

Approachable

I believe in listening first. Parents, teachers, students, and community members should feel comfortable bringing their concerns and ideas forward. Your voice matters, and I will work to make sure it is heard.

03

Visionary

I have a long-term vision for stronger schools and stronger students: strong academics, safe and welcoming environments, supported teachers and students, responsible spending, and informed, engaged parents working together.

04

Problem solver

I don’t believe in simply pointing out problems—I believe in solving them. For every issue, we should ask: What is wrong? What needs to change? And how will we know it worked? I will focus on practical solutions and measurable results.

05

Financial awareness

With a strong academic background in financial literacy and experience as part of a successful business, I understand responsible financial decisions. Every dollar should be spent thoughtfully, transparently, and in the best interests of all students.
