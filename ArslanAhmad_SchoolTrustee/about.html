MEET ARSLAN

Rooted in community.
Focused on results.

Arslan Ahmad is a proud resident of the King/Ward 1 community. Having called Kleinburg home and now residing in King, with family throughout Ward 1 and King City, his commitment to this community runs deep.

As an educated business professional and part of a successful family-run business, Arslan understands hard work, responsibility, accountability, and investing in the future of our kids.

He is running for School Trustee to ask the difficult questions, examine the evidence, and work toward better outcomes—so every student can reach their full potential.
