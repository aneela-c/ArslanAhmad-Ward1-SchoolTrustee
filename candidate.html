THE MISSION

Every child deserves
the best education.

It shouldn’t matter which school a child attends, what neighbourhood they live in, or how much their parents earn. Every student should have access to high-quality education, qualified teachers, appropriate classroom support, and the opportunity to succeed.

THE AGENDA

Four priorities
for better schools.

  1. 01

    Smaller classrooms

    Smaller classroom sizes can help teachers better understand their students, identify challenges earlier and create a stronger learning environment where every child has the opportunity to participate and thrive. I will advocate for appropriate staffing and additional support in schools and classrooms where it is needed most.

  2. 02

    Every student matters

    Every student deserves to be seen, supported and given the opportunity to reach their full potential. We need to identify students who are struggling early, provide the right support, and work with teachers and families to help students succeed. I would emphasize strengthening early support and communication between teachers, families and schools to help create the bridge of support each student requires.

  3. 03

    Strong schools, equal opportunities

    Every child deserves access to a high quality education, no matter which school they attend. We need to identify where schools and students need additional support, direct resources where they are needed most and make sure every school has the tools and support necessary to meet high standards. Putting clear measures in place will allow us to ensure every school is held to the same high level of excellence.

  4. 04

    Making every dollar count

    Education funding should always be focused on what matters most: our students. We need to spend responsibly and transparently, reduce unnecessary expenses where possible, and make thoughtful decisions about where funding can have the greatest impact on students, teachers and the classroom. Every program we fund should have a purpose and a way to measure whether it is working. We need to look at the results, understand what is making a difference for students, and make sure funding is directed towards what works.

When we invest in education, we invest in our future. Education is about so much more than academics—it is about empowering the whole child, nurturing their potential, building their confidence, and giving every student the opportunity to thrive.

I believe education should support the whole child—not just academically, but also by nurturing their physical, mental, emotional and social well-being. When we invest in the whole child, we build stronger students, stronger communities and a strong future.

WHO I AM AS A CANDIDATE

A neighbour who
shows up.

Your local neighbour

I don’t just represent this community—I live here. I care deeply about the people and families who call it home. The challenges our community faces matter to me because they affect my neighbours, my family, and the future of our children. That is why I am committed to making our schools stronger for every student.

Approachable

I believe in listening first. Parents, teachers, students, and community members should feel comfortable bringing their concerns and ideas forward. Your voice matters, and I will work to make sure it is heard.

Visionary

I have a long-term vision for stronger schools and stronger students: strong academics, safe and welcoming environments, supported teachers and students, responsible spending, and informed, engaged parents working together.

Problem solver

I don’t believe in simply pointing out problems—I believe in solving them. For every issue, we should ask: What is wrong? What needs to change? And how will we know it worked? I will focus on practical solutions and measurable results.

Financial awareness

With a strong academic background in financial literacy and experience as part of a successful business, I understand responsible financial decisions. Every dollar should be spent thoughtfully, transparently, and in the best interests of all students.
